# Who Is Solving What

## Thesis

Agent IAM is not a single product category yet. Pieces are emerging across edge
identity, fine-grained authorization, policy engines, workload identity,
agent-security tooling, and app-platform controls.

## Reader Promise

By the end of this chapter, a platform engineer should understand which
categories solve which part of the agent IAM problem, where the gaps remain, and
which vendors or open source projects are worth watching.
