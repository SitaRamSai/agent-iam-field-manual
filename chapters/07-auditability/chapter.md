# Chapter 7: Auditability & the Agent as a Principal

**Status:** outlined — target ship: Phase 2.
**Length target:** ~1800 words.

## Thesis (one sentence)

If you can't answer "what did the agent do, on whose behalf, with what
authority" — you don't have an agent, you have a liability.

## Outline

1. Audit primitives from classical IAM: who, what, when, on-behalf-of.
2. Agent-specific complications: tool calls, sub-agent delegation,
   retrieval reads, long-running tasks.
3. Log schema: minimum fields, retention, indexing.
4. The agent's audit trail as a debugging tool, not just compliance.
